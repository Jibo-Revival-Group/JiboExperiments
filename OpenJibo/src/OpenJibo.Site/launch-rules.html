    How launch rules work

    Uploaded rules are stored on your OpenJibo cloud under the robot's friendly name. When your robot sends a hotphrase launch turn, OpenJibo matches the spoken phrase against those rules and redirects to the skill named in each rule's {%skill='…'%} entity.

    Rules apply only to global launch listens (not in-skill CLIENT_NLU turns). Your robot must register with the same friendly name you enter above.
