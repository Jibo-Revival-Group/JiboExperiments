    How matching works

    When any robot sends a hotphrase launch turn, OpenJibo matches the spoken phrase against these rules and redirects to the skill in each rule's {%skill='…'%} entity.

    Example: GalleryRule = ($* open gallery {%skill='@be/gallery'%} $*);
